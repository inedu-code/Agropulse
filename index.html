From Leaf-Level to Field-Wide Intelligence

The Challenge: Traditional crop scouting is slow and incomplete. By the time nutrient deficiencies or disease are visible to the human eye, yield losses are already locked in — and harvest timing is often guesswork.

The AgroPulse Solution: Our computer vision AI analyzes multispectral imagery at the individual leaf level, detecting nutrient deficiencies, disease pressure, and water stress weeks before visible symptoms appear. Simultaneously, our yield forecasting models integrate satellite data, drone imagery, and soil sensor readings to predict harvest dates and volumes with 97% accuracy.
